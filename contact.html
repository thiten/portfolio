About me

Originally from Maceió, Alagoas, Thiago Tenorio is an illustrator trained at Quanta Academia de Artes. He has been working in the publishing market for three years, developing children's and educational book projects for companies such as IMEPH, Scriba, Saraiva, Saber, Somos, Asinha, among others. Greater than the responsibility of illustrating a children's book is the joy and pleasure of contributing to the education of new generations. Art educates.
